### On-Demand
- Pay per second/hour
- No commitment
- Best for short-term or unpredictable workloads

### Reserved Instances (RI)
- 1 or 3 year commitment
- Up to ~72% cheaper
- Steady workloads

### Savings Plans
- Commitment to **usage**, not instance type
- Flexible and cost-efficient

### Spot Instances
- Uses unused AWS capacity
- Up to ~90% cheaper
- Can be interrupted

### Dedicated Hosts
- Physical server dedicated to one customer
- Compliance or licensing needs
